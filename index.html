Conversion sequence

The conversion factor is the product of the step factors below. Steps are applied in the order time base, area base and input prefix, then molar mass, stoichiometry and GWP where required, and finally the output prefix. Each step shows the intermediate result and its unit. Hover a unit part to see which steps it affects.
