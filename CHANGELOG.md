## 1.0.0 - 20 Oct 2026

First release.

### Added

- Flux conversion for CO₂, CH₄, N₂O and H₂O: molar or mass amounts, molecule or element basis (C, N), and CO₂-equivalents for CH₄ and N₂O (IPCC AR6 GWP₁₀₀).
- Area units m², ha and km²; time units from s to yr (365 d).
- Conversion sequence with each factor and the intermediate value and unit after each step.
- SwissFluxNet reference sequences, checked against the published factors.
- Batch conversion of pasted spreadsheet columns. Headers, timestamp columns and −9999 missing values stay unchanged.
- Constants used: IUPAC 2007 atomic weights, molar masses, GWP₁₀₀.
- 3D model of the selected gas.
- Shareable links with the current units and value.
- Light and dark theme.
